Course Syllabus

  1. Introduction (slides)
  2. Logic
    1. Propositional Logic (slides, notes)
    2. Predicate Logic (slides, notes)
    3. Inference and Proofs (slides, notes)
    4. Optional: A Non-Expert's Gentle Introduction to Formal Mathematics and AI4Math (slides)
  3. Sets and Functions (slides, notes)
  4. Counting
    1. Basic Counting (slides, notes)
    2. Permutations and Combinations (slides, notes)
    3. Binomial Theorem (slides, notes)
    4. Inclusion-Exclusion (slides, notes)
  5. Probability
    1. Introduction to Probability (slides, notes)
    2. Conditional Probability (slides, notes)
    3. Bayes' Theorem (slides, notes)
    4. Random Variables (slides, notes)
    5. Variances and Concentration (slides, notes)
  6. Number Theory
    1. Basic Modular Arithmetic and the Euclidean Algorithm (slides, notes)
    2. Modular Inverses, Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT), and Modular Powers (slides, notes)
    3. Introduction to Cryptography (slides, notes, challenge_cipher.txt)
  7. Algorithms
  8. Induction and Recursion
  9. Graphs

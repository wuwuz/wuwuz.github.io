Hi, I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST).

My research focuses on data security, privacy, and AI governance, covering topics including secure and private data access and processing, AI content provenance, AI service auditing, and more. My research typically involves analyzing threat models, designing algorithms, and implementing systems for concrete security and privacy problems. My research contributions have been adopted in real systems by major industry partners.

Hiring

I am currently looking for PhD students and research interns to join my group at HKUST.

I am looking for students who are interested in related topics including cryptography, privacy-preserving computation, security and privacy in machine learning, etc. Please feel free to reach out to me via email.

Prospective students: Please do not send an AI-generated summary of my papers; it does not help your email get read. To increase your chance, tell me what is special about you and share some unique thought or idea of your own.
